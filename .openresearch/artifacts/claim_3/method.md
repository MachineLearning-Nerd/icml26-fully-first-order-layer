1. A symbolic certificate records each theorem premise and mechanically checks
   exponent composition against primary-source Theorem 1.5.
2. The released PyTorch FFOLayer runs on active nonlinear constraints
   `||y||_2 <= 0.8` over four tolerances and two seeds. An analytic projection
   derivative independently checks its hypergradient.
3. A first-hit stationarity calibration searches multiple fixed horizons rather
   than selecting a formula-derived budget.

`O~(epsilon^-1)` and must reject the claimed exponent because the total becomes
`epsilon^-4`. A second control flips the nonlinear oracle direction and must
reject its negative cosine.
